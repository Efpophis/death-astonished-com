A few times during the process of making albums for The Redeemer Project or my later stuff, I've encountered a few of these situations:

* I was never fully happy with a song because either the AI was just not capable at the time, or I didn't know what I was doing.
* I got a really good result, but it was still not a good fit for the album, style-wise.
* A particular song just didn't come together or happen and it was cut.
* I ran out of room on an album and had to cut something.

This album is an attempt to rectify some of that. It has a lot of remakes, some alternate versions, and even some brand-new stuff.
