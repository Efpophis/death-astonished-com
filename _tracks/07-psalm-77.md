notes: |
  <p>This is one of the many tracks from Seven that I wanted to go back and re-do. That era saw an upgrade to the AI that didn't really go so well. Sure, the audio quality was remarkably better, but the AI had this nasty habit of injecting an annoying "shimmer" into the songs. If you listen to Seven, you'll hear it in places. This remains a source of disappointment to me, because Seven was a really good album, content-wise, and it was the last Redeemer Project album. I did the best I could with what I had, but I wish it could have been better.</p>

  <p>This song, in particular, suffered from the Suno Shimmer more than the rest, especially in the last verse. So this version eliminates that.</p>

```text
[Intro]
[Instrumental]
I cry to God in anguish
I cry to God and He hears me.

[Verse 1]
In my day of distress,
I sought the LORD.
All through the night,
I stretched out my hands
And my soul refused to be consoled.
I sigh as I think of God, 
My spirit fades as I ponder Him.

[Verse 2]
You kept me from closing my eyes,
I was too distraught to even speak!
I thought of former times,
I remembered the years gone by

[Bridge]
Through the night I ponder in my heart.
As I reflect, my soul begs the question:

[Chorus]
Is Yahweh's rejection final?
Will He ever show grace again?
Is His faithful love gone forever?
Has His word gone silent for all time?
Does God forget to show mercy?
In anger, is His compassion lost?

[Interlude]
[Instrumental]

[Verse 3]
This is the source of my wounds,
The hand of God has lost its strength.
I remember all Your miracles,
I remember Your wonders from the past
I reflect on all You've done for me,
I ponder all Your deeds.

[Verse 4]
God, Your ways are holy!
What god is as great as Yahweh?
You are the God who works miracles,
All the nations confess Your power
By Your hand, you redeem Your people, 
The children of Jacob and Joseph.

[Bridge]
Through the night I ponder in my heart.
As I reflect, my soul begs the question:

[Chorus]
Is Yahweh's rejection final?
Will He ever show grace again?
Is His faithful love gone forever?
Has His word gone silent for all time?
Does God forget to show mercy?
In anger, is His compassion lost?

[Interlude]
[Instrumental]

[Verse 5]
When the waters saw You, oh God,
When the waters saw You, 
They writhed in anguish
The very depths shook with fear.
The clouds wept, the thunder roared
Your lightning lit the world,
The Earth trembled and shook.

[Bridge]
Through the night I ponder in my heart.
As I reflect, my soul begs the question:

[Chorus]
Is Yahweh's rejection final?
Will He ever show grace again?
Is His faithful love gone forever?
Has His word gone silent for all time?
Does God forget to show mercy?
In anger, is His compassion lost?

[Ending]
```
