```text
[Intro]
Hit'oreru, shikdu
(Awaken, be watchful)
Ki merivkhem ha-satan
(For your adversary, the devil)
Meshotet ka'ari sho'eg
(Roams like a roaring lion)
U-mevaqqesh et asher yivla.
(Seeking whom he may devour)

[Verse 1]
Exorcizamus te,
Omnis immundus spiritus,
Omnis satanica potestas,
Omnis incursio infernalis adversarii,
Omnis legio,
Omnis congregatio et secta diabolica.

[Verse 2]
Ergo, draco maledicte,
Et omnis legio diabolica,
Adjuramus te.
Cessa decipere humanas creaturas,
E isque aeternae
Perditionis venenum propinare.

[verse 3]
Vade, satana,
Inventor et magister omnis fallaciae,
Hostis humanae salutis.
Humiliare sub potenti manu Dei.

[Verse 4]
Fac ecclesiam tuam securi
Tibi facias libertate servire;
Te rogamus, audi nos.

[Ending]
He who endures to the end
Will be saved.
```
<div class="lyrics">
Translations:

The Hebrew Intro is translated by the whispers (and in parens)

The latin is roughly translated as follows:

[Verse 1]
We exorcise you,
Every unclean spirit,
Every satanic power,
Every assault of the infernal adversary,
Every legion,
Every congregation and sect of the devil.

[Verse 2]
Therefore, accursed dragon,
And every diabilical legion,
We solemnly command you,
Cease deceiving human creatures,
And offering them the poison of eternal destruction.

[Verse 3]
Begone, Satan!
Inventor and master of every deceit,
Enemy of human salvation.
Be humbled beneath
The Mighty hand of God!

[Verse 4]
Oh God, make secure Your church
That we may serve You in freedom.
We beseech You, hear us.
