notes: |
  <p>I've remade Centurion / The Henchman's Prayer so many times, it's getting ridiculous, really.</p>

  <p>The first version was too bright and lively, I thought, but it was still pretty darn good. The second was so much like the way we used to play it back in the day that I had to keep it, but I still wasn't thrilled with how the vocals were done. This one seems to fix most of that.</p>

  <p>It's ironic that the last line of each one is "it is finished."  Because who knows if this will actually be the last time I do this song.</p>
