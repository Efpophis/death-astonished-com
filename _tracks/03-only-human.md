  <p>This is how it starts. Small little compromises. Tiny, secret indulgence given to our favorite sin. No one knows, but it's not harmless. Something starts to change, and temptation becomes more and more appealing as faith, righteousness, and devotion are pushed to the back seat. The narrator just wants to blow off a little steam, have a little fun. Grace becomes cheap, and mercy seems inexhaustible, so why not?  We're only human, right? Nobody's perfect, right?  And slowly our attitude towards sin changes. It becomes something we allow ourselves, something we enjoy, and we stop seeing it as something that breaks our Father's heart.</p>

  <p>But every action has consequences, and they're coming.</p>
