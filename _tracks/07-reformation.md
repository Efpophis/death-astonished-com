notes: |
  <p>The word "repent" is often misunderstood. We take it to mean that we feel regret for a wrong we've done, and we apologize, ask forgiveness and move on. That's part of it, but it's not the whole story.</p>

  <p>The word used in the Bible for this is usually some form of "metanoia," which includes a complete change of mind and heart. True repentance isn't just apologizing for sin, it's making the attempt to abandon the sinful path altogether.</p>
