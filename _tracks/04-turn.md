notes: |
  <p>We tend to get lost when we lose focus, and that's exactly what happened to our Narrator (and to me). Turning our gaze and focus back towards Jesus ultimately makes everything else seem negotiable at most.</p>

  <p>I was heavily inspired by "Turn Your Eyes Upon Jesus," written by Helen Howarth Lemmel in 1922, but I wanted to give it my own twist and flavor. So, naturally, we have some Greek and some metal thrown into the mix. The intro is the well-known "Jesus Prayer" that I've used before - you should recognize that at this point!  And the Chorus is translated to English in the middle of the song.</p>
